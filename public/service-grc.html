OVERVIEW

Structured compliance, engineered properly

We help organisations build management systems that stand up to audit, align controls to real risk, and produce the evidence auditors expect - without checkbox-driven bureaucracy.

LionSec provides consultancy and readiness services only. We do not issue certifications or accreditations; certification is performed by independent, accredited certification bodies. Our role is to prepare your organisation so that assessment succeeds on merit.

ENGAGEMENT METHODOLOGY

How an engagement runs

  1. Assessment - Current-state review, scope confirmation and requirements mapping.
  2. Gap identification - Structured comparison against the target standard and related obligations.
  3. Risk treatment - Prioritised remediation plan with ownership and effort estimates.
  4. Policy & controls - Policy suite and control implementation support.
  5. Internal audit - Pre-assessment audit to surface weaknesses before the external audit.
  6. Management review - Governance cadence, KPIs and continual-improvement inputs.
  7. Certification readiness - Final evidence review and handover to the accredited certification body.
