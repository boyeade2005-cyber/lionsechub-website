How We Operate

Authorised engagements only

Every security engagement begins with written scope and authorisation. We do not test systems we are not permitted to test.

Scope-first delivery

Clear boundaries agreed before work begins - what is in scope, what is out, and how success is measured.

Evidence-driven reporting

Findings come with reproducible evidence, business impact context and remediation guidance - not vulnerability dumps.

Remediation + retest

Every engagement ends with a prioritised remediation plan and an optional validation retest.

Confidentiality & Data Handling

  • Client information is treated as confidential and handled on a need-to-know basis.
  • NDA available for all engagements before any sensitive information is exchanged.
  • We never ask for passwords, private keys or credentials through public forms - sensitive materials move through secure, agreed channels.
  • Engagement data is retained only as required for delivery, audit and legal purposes.
  • Form submissions are processed by Formspree under its own privacy terms; see our Privacy Policy.

Honest Capability Boundaries

  • We are a registered organisation (CAC BN 9458297) - registration is not a security certification.
  • We do not claim ISO certification, government clearance or vendor partnerships we have not verified.
  • Framework references (NIST, OWASP, ISO, MITRE ATT&CK) describe methodology alignment, not accreditation.
  • QKD and optical quantum work is delivered through qualified partners - we do not manufacture quantum hardware.
  • Defence-sector work is limited to lawful, authorised defensive cybersecurity.
