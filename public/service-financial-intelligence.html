OVERVIEW

Security where money and trust intersect

Financial institutions face layered threats: payment fraud, account takeover, business email compromise, insider abuse and API exploitation. This division combines technical security assessment with fraud-focused controls and lawful intelligence support - without claiming regulatory certification or law-enforcement authority.

Scope note: Financial-crime intelligence and OSINT investigations are conducted strictly from lawful, open sources. LionSec Hub does not claim regulatory certification, law-enforcement powers or guaranteed fraud outcomes; engagements are advisory, assessment and engineering work under formal scope.
