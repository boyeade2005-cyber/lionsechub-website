SECTOR CHALLENGES

Where financial institutions lose control

The attack surface of a modern bank spans legacy cores, open APIs, partner fintechs and long-lived cryptography.

FRAUD

Payment fraud & account takeover

Credential stuffing, OTP fraud and social engineering erode customer trust and inflate fraud losses faster than controls are upgraded.

BEC

Executive impersonation & BEC

Business email compromise targets payment approvals and vendor changes, bypassing technical controls through trusted channels.

API

Digital banking API exposure

Open banking and embedded finance multiply endpoints - each misconfigured API is a direct path to customer funds and data.

LEGACY

Legacy core-banking exposure

Mainframes and core systems run decades-old cryptography and protocols that cannot be patched without migration planning.

GRC

Regulatory pressure

CBN directives, PCI DSS for card data and the NDPA impose overlapping obligations that stretch thin compliance teams.

PQC

Harvest-now-decrypt-later

Long-lived financial data and payment cryptography are vulnerable to future quantum decryption of intercepted traffic.
