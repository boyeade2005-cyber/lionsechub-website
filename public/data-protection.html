OVERVIEW

Protecting data as a system, not a checkbox

Data protection fails when it is treated as policy alone. This division designs, assesses and hardens the technical and organisational controls that keep personal and sensitive data secure across its lifecycle - from classification and collection through storage, transfer and disposal.

Architecture-led protection

Data protection woven into systems, pipelines and cloud architectures rather than bolted on afterwards.

Regulatory clarity

Practical advisory on GDPR, Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 and ISO/IEC 27701 certification readiness.
