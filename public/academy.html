THE ACADEMY STORY

Why LionSec Academy

There is no single traditional path into cybersecurity. Our students arrive from teaching, administration, engineering, the humanities - and build real careers.

FROM OUR INTRO FILM - TRANSCRIPT

Speaker A

One of the most interesting things about cybersecurity is there's not a really traditional path. Everyone's come in in very different ways - especially folks who've been in the industry for longer. A lot of people have come from help desk, systems administration, but I've also talked to people who pivoted into cybersecurity from being an archaeologist and a librarian, which I think is really fascinating.

Speaker B

I was a middle school English writing teacher for five years, and I loved what I did, but it was very demanding. Honestly, it didn't pay very much. So I started to consider with my family other options. I decided, "Hey, why not cybersecurity in tech?" I was working full-time as a teacher, then at nighttime I was studying for the CompTIA Security+ exam - and I landed my first role as a security analyst.

Career changers are welcome. Our Foundations and Technical Fast-Track programmes are built for exactly this path.
