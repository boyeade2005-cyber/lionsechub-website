OVERVIEW

Assurance through verification.

This division combines offensive assessment with defensive operations. We test what attackers can reach, engineer detection that actually works, and validate that remediations close the gap - not just the ticket.

ENGAGEMENT METHODOLOGY

How Engagements Run

  1. Scoping & authorization - written scope, rules of engagement and sign-off before any testing begins.
  2. Assessment - testing and operations work performed to the agreed plan.
  3. Findings validation - each result reproduced, evidenced and prioritised by risk.
  4. Report - executive summary, technical detail and a remediation plan.
  5. Remediation - optional support through your team or ours.
  6. Retest / assurance - validation that fixes actually close the findings.
