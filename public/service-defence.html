ENGAGEMENT METHODOLOGY

How engagements run

  1. Authorisation & scoping - confirm legal basis, written authorisation and a precise, signed scope of work.
  2. Assessment & discovery - review systems, architecture, policies and threat environment within scope.
  3. Findings & remediation - documented, prioritised findings with actionable fixes.

  5. Reporting & handover - evidence-grade technical report and continuity recommendations.
