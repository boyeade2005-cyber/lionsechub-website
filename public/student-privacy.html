POLICY

Student Privacy & Information Protection

Student information is confidential. Access is restricted.

1. What we protect

LionSec Hub does not make students' private contact details available to prospective students, marketing contacts or unrelated third parties.

The following information is not published as part of student testimonials or programmes:

  • Personal telephone numbers and email addresses.
  • Private conversations and correspondence.
  • Payment records and financial information.
  • Identification documents and personal records.
  • Private academic assessments and individual performance records.

2. What may be published - with permission

Student names, photographs, professional profiles, project submissions and personal statements are published only with the appropriate permission.

Enrolment does not constitute consent to public disclosure. Participation in a training programme does not give LionSec Hub unrestricted permission to use a student's identity, personal information or work for promotional purposes.

3. Access control

Access to student records is restricted to authorised personnel who require the information for legitimate training, administration or support responsibilities.

4. Legal disclosure

Where disclosure is legally required, LionSec Hub will handle it in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

5. Verification requests

Prospective students, partners and employers may request verification of a student's enrolment or work. Such requests are handled through appropriate channels and only with the student's consent.

6. Your control

Students retain control over whether their full names, photographs, professional profiles or personal experiences are displayed publicly, and may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

7. Contact

Privacy and student-data questions: lionsechub@gmail.com · WhatsApp +234 810 754 9232

This policy may be updated as our programmes grow. For website data handling generally, see our Privacy Policy.
