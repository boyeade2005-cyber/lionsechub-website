ENGAGEMENT METHODOLOGY

How Engagements Run

  1. Authorisation & scoping - written authorisation, scope and rules of engagement agreed and signed.
  2. Reconnaissance - structured information gathering within authorised boundaries.
  3. Controlled testing - exploitation and simulation performed safely against agreed targets.
  4. Evidence validation - every finding reproduced, documented and risk-rated.
  5. Report - technical findings, attack narrative and remediation plan.
  6. Remediation & retest - validation that fixes close the findings.
